# JOYBOX visual system

The approved Georgian logo is the final identity. `public/brand/joybox-approved.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of the supplied original. Header, footer and story placements show its artwork proportionally without altering the Georgian lettering, colors or elements. A simplified open box/navy smile is used only for icons and schematic brand illustrations.

Colors: deep navy #102D57, darker room/nav background #081E39, yellow #FFCC20, cream #FFF7E9 and dark ink #152B48. Yellow is reserved for primary actions and brand emphasis. Warm light comes from the conceptual scene, not decorative glow effects. Body content uses cream/paper with restrained dividing lines.

Typography: locally hosted Noto Sans Georgian regular and extra-bold with `font-display: swap`. Georgian-first headings, generous line-height, fluid display sizes. Body copy stays readable at mobile sizes; compact labels are secondary. Rounded buttons use consistent spacing, SVG arrows and visible keyboard focus.

Hero follows the reference's left headline/right box composition, navy bedroom and warm practical light. Its original generated art is visibly labeled conceptual; it is not inventory photography. Mobile stacks text over the room scene. The curved cream how-it-works panel continues the reference composition.

Planned Mini/Classic/Mega formats are labeled upcoming with no invented price, age, stock or ratings. Actual product cards appear only from published catalog data. Empty, loading and error states are designed as part of the interface.

Assets: favicon.ico, favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, site.webmanifest and opengraph-image.png. See `public/brand/PROVENANCE.md` for attribution and generation prompt. Local screenshots are in `docs/qa`.
