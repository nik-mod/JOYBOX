# MyWonderBox assets

- mywonderbox/logo.webp and logo-compact.webp — approved artwork from `63692900-27ed-445f-a08e-9b42ebefba87.png`, resized for the site with a transparent outer backdrop.
- mywonderbox/box-blue.webp and box-pink.webp — approved packaging visuals from `Playful MyWonderBox Mystery Box.png` and `MyWonderBox Pink Wonder Gift Box.png`. The `-compact` copies serve smaller screens.
- mywonderbox/box-blue-detail.webp and box-pink-detail.webp — alternate gallery views from `ბიჭის ყუთი — Blue Gift Box.png` and `MyWonderBox Pink Mystery Gift Box.png`. Artwork, colors, and package proportions remain as supplied.
- mywonderbox/duo.webp and lifestyle.webp — `MyWonderBox Mystery Gift Duo.png` and the upper playroom photograph from `MyWonderBox Mystery Box Adventures.png`. Both have smaller responsive copies.
- mywonderbox/how-journey.mp4 and how-journey-mobile.mp4 — silent, seek-optimized versions of the supplied `gemini_generated_video_ea6240cc.mp4`. The mobile encode is 640 × 360. Both how-journey posters use the first frame of the matching encode.
- mywonderbox/home-unboxing.mp4 and its two posters — the supplied `gemini_generated_video_773090b6.mp4`, encoded for silent forward and reverse scrubbing on the homepage. The posters use its first frame.
- hero-compact.webp: resized copy of the conceptual hero for smaller devices and the unboxing detail.
- hero-concept.webp / hero-concept.png: approved bedroom concept image, created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-16 and used as the source for the homepage hero.
- hero-teddy/core.webp: continuous face, neck, bow and torso derived from the bedroom hero with the built-in image generation tool. Scroll motion composes this layer with separate plush limbs and the box interior.
- hero-teddy/opening.webp and opening-compact.webp: the resting frame rendered by the homepage animation on the original bedroom image, exported at desktop and mobile sizes.
- mywonderbox/orbit-navy-*.webp and orbit-berry-*.webp — the printable areas of `gashlilibiwisyuti.png` and `gashliliyutisgogo.png` mapped to the existing 22 × 20 × 9 cm demonstration model. Main print motifs retain their source proportions.
- orbit-models: locally served, optimized licensed toy assets for the orbital scene. See [model credits](orbit-models/credits.txt) for sources, licenses and changes. The rocket, molded puzzle cube, bracelets and paint set were modeled for this scene.
- story-play: responsive, transparent studio renders of the original rocket, puzzle cube, stacking rings, spinning top, camera and paint set, plus the CC0 toy car and teddy from the orbital showcase. See [model credits](orbit-models/credits.txt). The grain texture was generated procedurally.
- mywonderbox/journey-toys: responsive WebP studio renders of the orbital rocket, puzzle cube, stacking rings and spinning top, plus the rabbit, unicorn, dinosaur, car and blocks. See [model credits](orbit-models/credits.txt) for the source assets and licenses. The seated bear recomposes the existing hero-teddy torso and limbs into a standalone still.
- favicon.svg and raster app icons use an original open-carton and reveal-star mark in the MyWonderBox palette.
- opengraph-image.png — the supplied Mystery Gift Duo photograph resized to the 1200 × 630 sharing format.
- fonts: Noto Sans Georgian by Google, downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com, self-hosted. Font license: SIL Open Font License.

The original cinematic hero shows an open kraft carton with a navy smile and toy characters. This brand-neutral concept scene remains the source plate for the teddy animation.
