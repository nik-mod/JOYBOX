# JOYBOX assets

- joybox-approved.png: original official logo supplied by the project owner, copied byte-for-byte without modifications. Displayed with original design and lettering.
- joybox-header.webp and logo-background-mask.png: compact resampling of the approved artwork and a CSS mask derived from its edge-connected navy backdrop. The original is retained for official brand placements.
- joybox-mark.webp: trimmed transparent WebP combining the approved artwork with the existing backdrop mask.
- joybox-story.webp: web-sized conversion of the same approved logo artwork for the homepage brand section.
- hero-compact.webp: resized copy of the conceptual hero for smaller devices and the unboxing detail.
- hero-concept.webp / hero-concept.png: approved bedroom concept image, created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-16 and used as the source for the homepage hero.
- hero-teddy/core.webp: continuous face, neck, bow and torso derived from the bedroom hero with the built-in image generation tool. Scroll motion composes this layer with separate plush limbs and the box interior.
- hero-teddy/opening.webp and opening-compact.webp: the resting frame rendered by the homepage animation on the original bedroom image, exported at desktop and mobile sizes.
- joybox-unboxing.mp4: silent, seek-optimized 5.5-second excerpt of the owner-supplied `asd.mp4`. The closed and open WebPs come from its first and last frames. Used in the homepage How It Works chapter as a concept visual.
- box-navy.webp, box-berry.webp and their compact copies: responsive conversions of the owner-supplied `biwis.png` and `gogos.png`. These are packaging designs, presented independently of any stock or prices.
- orbit-navy-*.webp and orbit-berry-*.webp: face textures derived from the owner-supplied `gashlili biwis.png` and `gashlili gogos.png`. The guide markings were removed and the printable regions fitted to the 22 × 20 × 9 cm model. The plain panel in each sheet supplies the material for the rear carton wall.
- orbit-models: locally served, optimized licensed toy assets for the orbital scene. See [model credits](orbit-models/credits.txt) for sources, licenses and changes. The rocket, molded puzzle cube, bracelets and paint set were modeled for this scene.
- favicon.svg: original simplified flat box symbol derived from the approved logo's open yellow box and navy smile, reduced for small sizes. Raster icon family rendered from this SVG.
- opengraph-image.png: original approved logo, proportionally contained within a navy social sharing canvas.
- fonts: Noto Sans Georgian by Google, downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com, self-hosted. Font license: SIL Open Font License.

Hero prompt: Original cinematic brand concept background photograph for JOYBOX. Premium navy child's bedroom, warm lamp, ivory carpet, physically believable open corrugated kraft box on right half, natural teddy and wood toys, quiet left 45% for headline. Grounded objects, warm practical light, no text, no UI, no other brand artwork. Navy smile inspired by approved box symbol. Not product photography.
